I'm Robinn Mil Vinas, a first-year Computer Engineering student at Mapúa University, based in Caloocan City. I came into this course still unsure of exactly where I wanted to go after graduating from senior high school — my parents were the ones who encouraged me toward Computer Engineering, and that uncertainty ended up becoming part of my motivation: instead of coasting, I decided to work hard and figure out my direction along the way.

I'm interested in how hardware and software work together to create useful systems. I'm especially curious about embedded systems and IoT because they can be used to make everyday tasks easier and more efficient.

My goal is to improve my programming, problem-solving, and technical skills as I continue studying Computer Engineering. In the future, I want to work on projects that are practical, useful, and can help people.

  • Consistent honor student since Grade 5
  • Currently exploring organizations and activities related to Computer Engineering

Sheet 05 — Personal Journey LOG.ENTRY

My first year in CpE so far

A short reflection on how it's been, one year in.

Journal Entry Year 1

My journey as a first-year Computer Engineering student at Mapúa University has been challenging but meaningful. Adjusting to the fast-paced environment was one of the biggest challenges I experienced, especially when I had to keep up with different subjects and activities. There were times when I felt overwhelmed and had difficulty understanding some lessons, but these experiences taught me to manage my time and study more independently. As I continue my studies, I am slowly becoming more comfortable with the workload and learning from my mistakes. My experience so far has helped me grow not only academically but also as a person.
